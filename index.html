About The Book

The Brainware University Library is a central knowledge resource center supporting the academic and research needs of the university's students, faculty, and staff. Located in Prafulla Bhavan (University Building no. III), the main library, along with four departmental libraries, collectively houses a substantial collection of over sixteen thousand resources.
